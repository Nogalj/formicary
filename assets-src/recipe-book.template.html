The two chokepoints

The tree is not flat. Two items gate almost everything worth making, and both sit there on purpose — one is a metal tax on every piece of gear, the other costs a boss fight.

Read from src/generated/resources/data/formicary/recipe/ on branch ep2. Item art is the mod’s own. The three vanilla ingredients — iron ingot, stick, sugar — are drawn here as original stand-ins rather than shipped Minecraft textures.
