# Changelog

All notable changes to Formicary are recorded here. Format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/); versioning follows
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

The reasoning behind these changes — every design decision and play-test revision — lives
in [`docs/DECISIONS.md`](docs/DECISIONS.md).

## [1.0.0] — 2026-08-23

First public release. Minecraft 1.21, NeoForge 21.0.167.

### The dimension

- An ant-colony dimension entered by throwing an ender pearl at a savanna anthill.
- Three stacked soil tiers with their own terrain, generated colonies with chambers, a
  larder and a royal depth, plus resin seams, amber pockets and self-lighting fungal
  gardens.
- Crops can be planted and farmed inside the dimension.

### Ants

- Worker, soldier and ender ants, larvae, and the queen.
- Colony-wide anger: provoking the colony in one chamber brings soldiers elsewhere.
- The deep tiers are hostile without provocation.

### Taming

- Steal and raise a larva to imprint it on you.
- **Tamed workers** harvest crops — including pumpkins, melons and modded crops — and
  deliver them to a chest you bind them to.
- **Tamed soldiers** fight alongside you and hold a post.

### The queen

- Three-phase boss fight with burrowing, an acid-spit ranged attack and a frenzy roar.
- Drops the **Royal Pheromone Gland** and the **Queen's Crest**.

### Items and blocks

- **Pheromone Horn** — calls two allied soldiers to fight for you for a minute. They wear
  the tamed soldier's markings so you can pick them out of a fight.
- **Trail Pheromone** — lights the route you actually walked, so it is always traversable.
- **Chitin armour**, and the **Pincer Sword** and **Mandible Pickaxe** at Royal tier, above
  netherite and gated behind a Queen's Crest.
- **Chitin Plate**, the intermediate every piece of gear routes through.
- Three colony foods: Honeyed Comb, Fungal Cake and the Royal Jelly Treat.
- Building blocks: resin, polished resin, amber glass, brood comb, packed soil bricks and
  hardened soil tiles, each with stairs and slabs.
- 26 recipes in total — see [`docs/recipe-book.html`](docs/recipe-book.html).

### Sound

- Every mob voice is a named sound event, so any of them can be replaced by a resource
  pack without touching the mod. Worker, soldier and queen ship with recorded audio;
  the rest redirect to vanilla sounds.

### Under the hood

- 100 headless GameTests covering taming, harvesting, the boss fight, worldgen, loot and
  placement rules.
- All textures, mob models, mob voices and the mod icon are generated by scripts in
  `assets-src/` — there are no hand-drawn assets in the repository.
